Excursions and activities in Iceland

Below is a non-exhaustive list of excursions and activities available in Iceland. Depending on your program and time spent in each region, it may not be necessary to do all of them. We advise you to not to have “eyes bigger than your stomach” and do not hope us to include them all! If you wish to embellish your stay with many activities, consider extending the length of your trip rather than overloading your daily schedule. Ask us for advice! There are also many other possibilities! Do not hesitate to send us your wishes and we will be able to suggest alternatives to you!

In all cases: Reservations are highly recommended. If you reserve an activity through us, we guarantee its full refund if it should be cancelled by the provider (inclement weather or otherwise).

    • Perlan, wonders of Iceland - All year round

    Perched on the hill overlooking the city centre, Perlan represents the old hot water tanks for the city. Converted into a museum, you will find on site exhibitions related to Icelandic geology, bird life and marine life in Iceland, a planetarium dedicated to the Northern Lights, an exhibition on water and on glaciers and, icing on the cake. the gateau, an artificial ice cave made of real ice, cut from one of the cisterns to give you the experience of visiting the interior of a glacier. A comprehensive exhibition on Iceland and its wonders. A café and restaurant are available on site. Free shuttle from the city centre.

    • Place : Reykjavík – Close to the domestic Airport.
      Duration : As you wish!
      Supervision : Self-guided tour, interactive panels in English
